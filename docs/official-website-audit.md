## 1. Executive Summary
P.S.V College of Engineering and Technology (PSVCET) is an Autonomous Engineering Institution affiliated with Anna University, Chennai, approved by AICTE, accredited with NAAC 'A' Grade, and accredited by the NBA for Computer Science & Engineering. This audit provides an exhaustive inventory of the structure, content, navigation hierarchy, statutory disclosures, and digital services on `psvcet.ac.in` to guide the complete modern migration into the React + TypeScript application while strictly preserving our locked design baseline.

## 3. Site Navigation & Page Taxonomy

### 3.1 Top Utility Strip
- `IQAC`: Internal Quality Assurance Cell composition, AQAR reports, meeting minutes, and quality benchmarks.
- `NAAC`: Accreditation certificates, SSR documents, Criterion 1-7 metrics, and peer team reports.
- `NBA`: B.E. CSE Accreditation certificates, SAR, compliance reports, and outcome-based education (OBE) rubrics.
- `Mandatory Disclosure`: AICTE Mandatory Disclosure (Form 2024-25), land/building details, faculty lists, and fee charts.
- `ERP Portal`: Student & faculty academic management, internal assessments, and attendance logging.
- `Bus Route`: Comprehensive transport directory across Krishnagiri, Hosur, Dharmapuri, Tirupattur, and Pochampalli.
